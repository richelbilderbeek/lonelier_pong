# FAQ

## What is the history of this game?

The name and gameplay of 'Lonelier Pong' is inspired on
[Thijs van Beers](https://github.com/thijsvb)'
smash hit
[LonelyPong](https://github.com/thijsvb/thijsvb.github.io/tree/master/LonelyPong).

Lonelier Pong tried to top the loneliness and sadness of the original.
